Five ways to pay for care

There's no single right answer — the best option depends on your health, assets, and goals. Here's the short version of each.

01

Do Nothing

Pro: No cost or planning today.

Con: Leaves the full risk — and often the full burden of care — on your family.

02

Self-Insure

Pro: Full control, no premiums, no underwriting.

Con: Fully exposed to duration and inflation risk — can erode a lifetime of savings.

03

Traditional LTC Insurance

Pro: Highest leverage — benefits far exceed premiums paid.

Con: "Use it or lose it," and premiums aren't guaranteed.

04

Hybrid: Life + LTC

Pro: Unused benefit passes to heirs as a death benefit.

Con: Requires more upfront capital; often lower LTC benefit than traditional.

05

Hybrid: Annuity + LTC

Pro: Easier underwriting; unused value can pass to heirs.

Con: Requires a lump sum; annuity value reduces as care benefits are paid.
