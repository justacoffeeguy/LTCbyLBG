A note from Les

Why I do this work

I've been in financial services since 1984, and for the last 20+ years I've specialized in long-term care planning. Before that, I spent nearly two decades as a financial fiduciary here in the Chicago area, helping families build retirement plans they could actually trust.

In that time, I've sat with families who planned ahead and lived out their 80s and 90s with dignity, their savings and relationships intact. I've also sat with families who didn't, and watched a healthy spouse go broke covering care for the other. That gap is the whole reason I do this work.
