About Les

Twenty years of one focus.

Not retirement planning in general. Not investments. Just long-term care — understanding it, planning for it, and helping families choose the right way to cover it.

Photo of Les — swap in a real headshot here

Twenty years ago, in conversation after conversation with people approaching retirement, I kept seeing the same blind spot. People had a plan for the market, a plan for their income, a plan for their taxes — but no plan at all for the one thing statistically most likely to derail all of it: needing long-term care.

So I made that my focus. Not as one more product I offer alongside everything else, but as the entire practice. I've spent the years since helping people understand what long-term care actually costs, what triggers the need for it, and which of the several ways to pay for it actually fits their health, their assets, and their goals.

I had one client who needed care for eleven years. Between 2007 and 2018, she went through more than $1.3 million of her own savings — everything she had, except her condominium. She had planned well in every other way. Long-term care was the one piece she hadn't gotten to yet. That gap is the reason this work matters to me.
