About Les

Advice built on experience.

More than 40 years in financial planning shaped how Les approaches long-term care today.

Retirement isn't just about building wealth. It's about protecting the life you worked to create.

I spent more than 40 years helping individuals and couples make financial decisions with confidence. Before founding Personal Financial Strategies in 1996, I was a Vice President at a major Wall Street investment firm. That work taught me something I still lead with: accumulating wealth is only half of it. Protecting it is the other half.

I retired from the firm in 2022 and came back to the profession with one focus - helping people prepare for the possibility of long-term care. One unexpected health event can undo a lifetime of careful planning, and this isn't really an insurance decision. It's a family one. It touches your independence, your savings, and the people closest to you.

I also wrote The Big Picture Retirement Planning: From 30,000 Feet because I've always believed informed clients make better decisions. My job is to educate first, listen carefully, and help you understand your options.

As an independent advisor, my loyalty is to you, not to a carrier. When coverage makes sense, I compare solutions from leading companies and recommend one based on your health, goals, family, and budget. My commitment isn't to sell a policy. It's to help you make the right call.

Planning ahead isn't about expecting the worst. It's about keeping your independence, protecting your savings, and easing what your family would otherwise carry alone.

Traditional coverage, LTC + life, or LTC + annuity - your health, assets, goals, and budget decide, not a product menu.

Honest either way

If you don't need a policy, or your current one is still the better choice, I'll tell you directly.
